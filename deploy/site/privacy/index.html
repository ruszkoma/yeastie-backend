Yeastie Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Yeastie is a mobile app for home brewers that generates beer recipes and guides you through brew day. This policy explains what personal data the app and its server process, why, and what choices you have.

In short: we keep your account details and your brewing data so the app works on all your devices. We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is CProjekt, Oblęgorska 3, 01-472 Warszawa, Poland.

Contact for all privacy matters: contact@cprojekt.pl.

2. What data we process

DataDetailsWhere it is kept
Account Email address, first name, account identifier, sign-in method (email and password, Google or Apple). Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it. Firebase Authentication (Google), your device
Brewing data What you enter or create in the app: recipes, batches and their measurements and notes, inventory, equipment and water profiles, your own beer styles, app settings, and the count of recipes saved from the generator. Your device and our server
Subscription Trial start and end dates, the Google Play purchase token, the chosen plan, subscription state and expiry date, and a one-way hash of your account identifier. We never receive your card number or other payment details; payments are handled entirely by Google Play. Our server; a copy of your premium status on your device
Technical data IP address, date and time, and the address of each request sent to our server, recorded in server logs. Firebase Authentication also records the IP address and device information of sign-ins to protect accounts. Our hosting provider, Google

The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. Brew-day and fermentation reminders are scheduled on your device and are not sent through any server.

3. Why we process it and on what legal basis

  • To provide the app (creating your account, signing you in, keeping your data in sync between devices, restoring it on a new device, providing the trial and premium features): performance of our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To verify purchases and keep your subscription status correct: performance of the contract, and our legal obligations related to accounting and tax where they apply (Article 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR).
  • To keep the service secure (server logs, preventing abuse, preventing one purchase from being used by several accounts): our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Who receives your data

We use the following service providers, who process data on our behalf or as independent providers of their own services:

  • Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC): Firebase Authentication for sign-in, Google Play for purchases, and Google Cloud for subscription status notifications. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple, only if you choose Sign in with Apple. See the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Netstrefa (Poland), the hosting provider of our server and database.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose data when the law requires it.

5. Where your data is processed

Our server and database are located in the European Union. Google may process account and purchase data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and brewing data: until you delete your account.
  • Items you delete in the app are marked as deleted so the deletion reaches your other devices; they are removed for good when you delete your account.
  • Technical records used to apply changes exactly once: 30 days.
  • Purchase tokens: after you delete your account they stay on our server without any link to you, only so the same purchase cannot be attached to another account.
  • Server logs: for a limited period set by our hosting provider. Backup copies made by the hosting provider may contain deleted data for a short time until they are overwritten.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: More → Account → Delete account. This removes your account and all your data from our server and from the device.

If you cannot use the app, write to contact@cprojekt.pl from the email address of your account and we will delete the account and its data within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions) before you delete the account.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. To use any of these rights, contact us at the address above. We answer within one month.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO); in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office.

If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law: we do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it, and we will not treat you differently for using your rights.

9. Security

All communication between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). The server accepts requests only with a valid sign-in token and returns only the data of the signed-in account. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will inform you and the authorities of a data breach when the law requires it.

10. Children

Yeastie is about brewing beer and is intended only for adults of legal drinking age in their country. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date. If the changes are significant we will also tell you in the app.
