Brew the beer you can taste in your head

Yeastie is an Android app for homebrewers. Pick a style, describe the flavor with sliders, and get a complete recipe and process matched to your own brewhouse, fermenter and water.

Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • Recipe generatorChoose a beer style and set malt, hop and yeast character. Yeastie picks the yeast, grain bill, hops and water treatment and plans the mash and fermentation.
  • Match score and warningsEvery recipe shows how closely it hits the style and your flavor targets, and warns you when your equipment cannot do what the style needs.
  • Brew day modeStep-by-step guidance with timers and reminders that keep working in the background.
  • Batch logGravity readings with a chart, volumes, notes, and brewhouse efficiency learned from your previous batches.
  • InventoryLots, best-before dates and hop alpha acids. Ingredients are deducted automatically after brew day.
  • Works offlineEverything runs on your phone. Your data syncs to your account and comes back when you sign in on a new device.

The same choices always give the same recipe: Yeastie uses transparent brewing calculations, not guesswork.

Help

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the Google Play app: profile picture → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Yeastie → Cancel. Premium stays active until the end of the paid period.

I paid, but the app shows the free plan

Open the Premium screen in the app and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in to Yeastie with the same account you used when you subscribed.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Sign in with the same account. Your recipes, batches, inventory and profiles are downloaded automatically.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: More → Account → Delete account. This removes your account and all your data. If you cannot use the app, write to us from the email address of your account. Details are in the Privacy Policy. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel it in Google Play first.
